praxio — architectural visualisation & design studio in Bali

Practical design solutions. praxio is the studio of Justin Lawless, an architectural visualisation artist and designer based in Bali, Indonesia, working with architects, developers, interior designers and private clients worldwide. Still renders, animated walkthroughs, 3D modelling and concept design, with design input at every stage. Get a free instant quote in 1 minute.

What I offer

  • Visualisation: still renders, animated walkthroughs, and full project presentations for print or for PowerPoint video display.
  • 3D modelling: 3D modelling from CAD / plans, sketches or photos; organic or parametric 3D modelling.
  • Design: AI-assisted iterative concept designs, masterplan development for large projects, interior design mood boards and material selection.
  • Drawings & sourcing: detailed floorplans, elevations and sections; sourcing for materials, furniture and fittings.
  • Revisions at every stage: unlimited revisions within the agreed scope, with feedback at concept & layout, materials & look, lighting & atmosphere, and final images.

How a project starts

Projects can start from a full brief (plans or CAD), from reference images, or from just an idea. Concept design and 3D modelling are included on request. The online instant quote gives a price range in about a minute; the exact fixed price follows after review.

Background

Justin studied 2D animation and 3D modelling and began his career at a AAA video game studio, then brought drawing, digital painting and 3D sculpting into architecture and archviz. He has worked for more than 18 years creating images of designs that have been built. His specialty is rapid prototyping of designs and detailed, descriptive visuals and technical drawings, now with AI-assisted workflows for faster results at lower cost. Tools: 3ds Max, Corona Renderer and AI-assisted workflows.
